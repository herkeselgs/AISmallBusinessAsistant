# YC Metrics — what to track before July 27

Live, editable version (localStorage + copy-summary): **https://folvra.com/yc**

YC funds **evidence users love it**, not code. Track these daily. The bar isn't hitting every
target — it's a steep, believable curve with real customer quotes behind it.

## Numbers (update daily)
| Metric | Why it matters | 3-week target |
|---|---|---|
| Businesses contacted | Shows hustle / top of funnel | 300 |
| Real conversations | Qualified interest | 60 |
| Demos booked | Pipeline | 25 |
| Demos completed | Conversion quality | 20 |
| Pilots started | Product in real hands | 10 |
| Paying customers | The number that matters most | 3+ |
| Leads processed by Folvra | Usage / engagement | 200 |
| Estimates booked | Value delivered | 40 |
| Pipeline rescued ($) | Dollarized ROI | $25,000 |

## Words (capture verbatim)
- **Testimonials** — paste as they come in.
- **Strongest quotes** — the 3 one-liners that make a partner lean in. Get name + business + permission.
- **Biggest objections heard** — tally them; the top one is your next fix.
- **Product changes from feedback** — "we heard X, shipped Y in 2 days." Log every one; this is the
  single most YC-impressive artifact you can build.

## What the YC story should sound like
"Home-service owners lose $45k–$120k/year to slow lead follow-up — 78% of customers hire whoever
replies first, and the average business takes 47 hours. We put an AI employee on their inbox that
replies in under 60 seconds and follows up until the estimate is booked. In 3 weeks we went from 0 to
{N} pilots and {M} paying, processed {L} leads, and rescued ${R} in jobs. Here's an owner: '{quote}.'"

## The weekly rhythm
- Every Friday: update all numbers, pick the top objection, ship one change that answers it, and add
  a new quote. That cadence *is* the traction narrative.
