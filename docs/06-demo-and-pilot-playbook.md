# Demo & Pilot Playbook

All scripts are copy-pasteable at **https://folvra.com/founder**. This is the reference copy.

## 5-minute demo
1. **Hook (30s):** "Whoever follows up with a new lead first usually wins — 78% of the time. Most
   contractors take hours. Let's race that." Ask for one real/typical lead.
2. **Replay (2m):** Paste it at folvra.com. Show: job type + urgency detected → reply in their
   voice → follow-up + move-toward-booked. "That's what your customer gets at 11pm — in 40 seconds."
3. **Spam (30s):** Run the "Vendor spam" sample. "It won't send junk to your customers."
4. **Control (30s):** /dashboard → Approve-first. "Nothing goes out without your OK. Autopilot when
   you trust it."
5. **Math (30s):** "What's a job worth to you? One saved job = ~6 months of Folvra."
6. **Close (30s):** "Free 14-day pilot, live tomorrow. If it books you one job, you keep it. Fair?"

## Demo checklist
- Before: folvra.com + /dashboard open; know their trade + job value; have one of their leads.
- Close with: business email, best number, one lead source to point Folvra at; book setup on the spot.
- After: send the post-demo follow-up within 10 min; log everything.

## Cold call (30s)
"Hi {First}, {You} — 20 seconds. I help {trade} businesses stop losing jobs to slow follow-up on new
leads. Someone messages you at 2pm, you're on a job, they book someone else. I built Folvra — replies
to every lead in under a minute, follows up until they answer, helps book the estimate. Can I show you
what it'd say to one of YOUR leads? 5 minutes, pilot's free — saves you a job, you keep it. Fair?"

## Voicemail (then text immediately)
"Hi {First}, {You} — I built a tool that replies to your new leads in under a minute and follows up
until they book. I'll text you a 30-second link to see it on your own lead. Free pilot if it's useful.
{You}, {Number}."

## SMS / DM
"Hey {First} — saw you run {Company}. When an after-hours website/Angi lead comes in while you're on a
job, how's it handled? I built a tool that auto-replies in <60s and follows up until they book. Run it
on one of your real leads? No pitch. https://folvra.com"

## Cold email sequence
- **E1 `the lead you missed last night`:** 78% hire whoever replies first; you take hours. Folvra
  replies <60s, follows up, helps book — off your existing inbox (Yelp/Angi/Thumbtack too). "Want me
  to run it on one of your past leads? 5 min."
- **E2 (+2d) `re: the lead you missed`:** 40-sec version + folvra.com. "Forward me one lead email and
  I'll show you what Folvra would've sent."
- **E3 (+4d) `should I close this out?`:** "Even one saved job/month covers it. Reply 'show me' for a
  free 14-day pilot."

## Objections
- "I answer my own leads." → "This is for the ones you can't get to — after-hours, on a roof."
- "Sounds like a robot?" → "That's why it's text/email. Let me show you a reply on your own lead."
- "Already use Jobber/HCP." → "Keep it — Folvra just makes sure every lead gets a fast reply + follow-up."
- "Send me info." → "Lands better in 5 min on your own lead. I'll text the link and we watch it now."
- "No time to set up." → "That's on me. Send me one lead email, live tomorrow. Pilot's free."

## Post-demo follow-up (within 10 min)
"Great talking, {First}. Your free Folvra pilot is set up for {Company}, in approve-first mode —
nothing goes out without your OK. Try it on this week's leads; if it saves a job it's paid for itself.
I'll check in Friday."

## Pilot onboarding
1. Folvra now watches your leads; every new one gets a reply drafted in <60s.
2. Approve-first: I send you each draft, you say "send" or tweak. Nothing goes out without you.
3. After a few days we flip on autopilot; it only pings you for anything sensitive.
4. Your job: forward me leads to handle, tell me when a reply feels off.
5. 14-day goal: never let a lead sit; book one job you'd have lost.

## Asking for payment (after a win)
"You booked {N} jobs from leads Folvra caught in two weeks. Keep it going? $99/mo, cancel anytime —
I'll send a payment link." Hesitation → "What would make this a no-brainer?" then deliver exactly that.

## Asking for a testimonial (right after a win)
"Quick favor — that job Folvra just booked, would you give me a one-liner I can share? 'Folvra booked
me a $__ job I'd have missed.' Your name + business. Huge help." Then ask for a referral (free month).

## Best-fit customer
Home-service business, owner-run, **10+ inbound leads/month**, already advertising (Google/Angi/
Thumbtack), on jobs during the day. Remodelers, HVAC, roofers, plumbers, electricians, landscapers,
cleaners.
